Privacy Policy for Robot Lockout

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Robot Lockout ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android. This page explains what information the game uses.

Contact: 1234kadosh@gmail.com

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, hint count and purchases are stored on your device. The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name or email. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may back up this game data to your Google account and restore it on a new phone. Google handles that backup; we do not receive it.

Analytics

The game uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to learn how the game is played: which screens and levels are opened, how long levels take, which buttons are used, app version, and general device information such as device model, Android version and country. Each install gets a random ID that is not linked to your name. This helps fix hard levels and bugs.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) sends a crash report: what the game was doing at the moment it crashed (a technical stack trace), app version, device model, Android version and a random installation ID. This helps us fix crashes.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and other device information to show and measure ads, and to limit fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings -> Google -> Ads). Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Purchases

In-app purchases (remove ads, unlock levels, hint packs) are processed by Google Play. The game does not see or store your payment details.

Sharing

Data is shared only with the Google services above (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play). We do not sell your data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and data deletion

Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device. For questions or to ask about data tied to your random install ID, email the contact above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
